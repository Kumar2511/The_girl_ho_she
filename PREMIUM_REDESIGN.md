## Conversion Metrics & Best Practices Applied

1. **Visual Hierarchy**: Important elements sized and positioned for impact
2. **Color Psychology**: Rose gold creates luxury, Champagne adds elegance
3. **Social Proof**: 50K+ customers, 4.9★ rating, verified reviews
4. **Urgency**: Limited items, pre-orders, stock indicators
5. **Trust Building**: SSL badges, return policy, quality guarantees
6. **Accessibility**: Semantic HTML, alt text, proper contrast ratios
7. **Mobile First**: Responsive design that works seamlessly on all devices
8. **Performance**: Optimized images, smooth animations, fast loading
